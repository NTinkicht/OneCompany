# Quality Waivers

A waiver is visible technical debt with an owner and expiry, never a silent bypass.

Every waiver records the violated rule, rationale, linked issue, owner, risk level, scope, compensating control, creation date and expiry. Permanent waivers are forbidden. Expired waivers block merge.

High/critical-risk waivers require independent approval; critical security/privacy/safety waivers require human approval.

Repeated waiver renewal is a signal that the policy, architecture or debt-remediation plan needs explicit review.
