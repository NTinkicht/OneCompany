# Review Standard

Review is evidence-based challenge, not approval theatre.

The independent reviewer checks the exact current SHA and challenges requirement correctness, unhandled cases, architecture, security/privacy, data integrity, tests, risk treatment, maintainability, documentation and operational safety according to impact.

The material author cannot be the sole independent gate. A head change after PASS invalidates the exact-head verdict until the required checks/review are refreshed.

Review comments distinguish blocking correctness/safety findings from non-blocking improvements. Style preferences already enforced deterministically should not consume reviewer attention.
